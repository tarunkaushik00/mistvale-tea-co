# Mistvale Tea Co. 🌿

**Hill-grown tea, honestly made.**

Mistvale Tea Co. is a premium tea e-commerce website designed around a calm, earthy and minimal visual identity. It showcases a curated collection of teas with warm product photography, a nature-inspired colour palette and a smooth shopping experience.

## 🌐 Live Website

**Live Demo:** [Visit Mistvale Tea Co.](YOUR_LIVE_WEBSITE_URL)


## 📖 About the Project

Mistvale Tea Co. is a modern online tea store concept created to provide an elegant and user-friendly shopping experience for tea lovers in India.

The website focuses on:
- Premium and minimal brand presentation.
- Easy product discovery and browsing.
- Responsive, mobile-first design.
- Simple shopping interactions.
- Clean product information and transparent pricing.

## ✨ Features

### Shopping Experience
- Product catalogue.
- Product search.
- Category filters and sorting.
- Product quick-view functionality.
- Wishlist with Local Storage.
- Shopping cart with quantity controls.

### Cart and Checkout
- Add and remove products.
- Quantity management.
- Coupon functionality.
- Discount calculations.
- Free-shipping threshold calculation.
- Checkout form.

### Additional Features
- Pincode-based delivery availability check.
- Customer reviews section.
- FAQ accordion.
- Newsletter form validation.
- Responsive navigation.
- Mobile-friendly interface.

## 🎨 Design & Branding
